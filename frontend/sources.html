Knowledge Sources

The assistant answers only from these three documents (121 indexed passages in total). Page numbers in answers are the page numbers of the indexed PDF files.

National tree-planting guideline

Guidelines and Lists of Suitable Tree Species for Plantation in Different Silvo-Ecological Zones of Pakistan (2023). Ministry of Climate Change and Environmental Coordination, Government of Pakistan.

Pakistan-wide guidelineGuideline66 passages
What it contains
General planting guidance, species lists for the silvo-ecological zones of each province, urban landscaping tables, and annexes on bird food and pollinators and on saline and waterlogged soils.
Parts used most
Sindh zone tables (pages 46-48), urban landscaping tables (pages 64-75), Annex 2 (page 76), Annex 3 (page 77).
Reach
It is not specific to Thatta. The Sindh section gives six zones for the province and does not say which zone Thatta belongs to.

Halophyte survey in District Thatta

Kaleri et al. (2017). Distribution of Halophyte Plants in Relation to Properties of Salt-Affected Soils of District Thatta. Journal of Basic & Applied Sciences, 13, 85-90.

Measured in District ThattaField survey34 passages
What it contains
Soil analyses (EC, pH, organic matter, ions) from salt-affected sites in Thatta, with the halophyte plants collected there and their ion contents.
How answers treat it
As measurements. It is a survey of which plants grew where, not a planting recommendation or a tolerance rating.

Salinity and drought experiments on Thar species

Rajpar et al. (2018). Salinity and Drought Stress Tolerance of Some Plant Species of Thar Desert, Pakistan. Pakistan Journal of Botany, 50(2), 465-469.

Sindh region, not necessarily ThattaPot experiments21 passages
What it contains
Controlled pot experiments on seedlings of four indigenous Thar species (Kandi, Kumbhat, Jar and Rohira) under salinity and under drought.
How answers treat it
As a study of which species were tested and how they ranked, not as a planting recommendation. Salinity and drought rankings are reported separately.

What the documents do not say

  • Which silvo-ecological zone Thatta belongs to. The guideline does not mention Thatta, so the assistant answers "Evidence unavailable" or "Partial evidence" for such questions.
  • Numeric salinity limits for the Low, Medium and High levels in Annex 3. A Low level does not mean a species is unsuitable.
  • Planting recommendations for Thatta from the two research papers. They describe a survey and pot experiments.

A Punjab Forest Department species list was collected earlier but is not used for Thatta answers. Passage counts are as of the last time the knowledge base was built.
